## What was claimed, and what the audit found

The original stack claimed a millimetre-scale monocular depth error, a closed-form
inverse-kinematics solver with full convergence, a high ten-task success rate, a high
conveyor-sorting rate, and a sub-30 ms emergency stop. The audit found four classes of
measurement failure:

1. **Null measurements.** The conveyor benchmark caught every TF exception and returned a
   zero pose, then differenced two zero poses to obtain a velocity.
2. **Provenance mismatch.** The reachability logs cited a commit that did not contain the
   script that produced them, and the solver had been rewritten between result sets.
3. **Fallback fabrication.** The E-STOP benchmark published to a topic it also subscribed
   to, never moved an arm, and added a constant to the command latency when no physical stop
   was observed.
4. **Hard-coded outcomes.** Two scripted-baseline tasks used literal pose deltas, and the
   solver's orientation residual was set to zero by convention.

The full defect list and dispositions are in `AUDIT.md`.

## Honest interpretation

- **Reachability is reproducible**, but the historical gap was a solver/model revision, not
  an algorithmic improvement. The old solver was solving a different, inconsistent kinematic
  problem.
- **The conveyor is negative.** The plant realizes the command with a nearly constant offset,
  not a scale. The visual belt is effectively stationary and the rollers spin at an
  implausible rate. A scale correction cannot repair this.
- **The safety-agent stop is negative.** The agent publishes a 4-joint, velocity-free halt
  that the controller rejects because partial goals are disabled. The direct command path
  stops; the safety agent does not.
- **N4 is conditional.** The gate removes execution failures from the plans it accepts, but
  it rejects 53% of targets, rescues 1 baseline failure, and loses 6 baseline successes.
  Intention-to-treat success is lower. No practical improvement is claimed.

## Not executed (declared before evaluation)

- **N1** depth calibration: depth model package and checkpoint absent.
- **N2** uncertainty-aware grasp gating: grasp attempts blocked by the gripper defect.
- **N3** scene-match + calibrated LLM confidence: not executed within the available budget.
- **P0-WD** 2.0 s LLM watchdog: no such watchdog exists in the code; the only configured
  timeout is 90 s.
- **Phase 3** learned policies (LeRobot/ACT/SmolVLA): no CUDA device.
- **Phase 4** human inter-annotator agreement: no independent human annotators.
- **Phase 7** hardware: no robot, ESP32, or serial device.
- **Full detector run**: no committed checkpoint and no provenance-clean labelled dataset.

These are reported as not executed, never simulated.

## Known defects that bound the claims

- The conveyor plant is kinematic, so workpieces cannot tip, rotate, or jam.
- The gripper is a revolute two-finger mechanism with non-mirrored pads; only a top grasp is
  kinematically feasible. Grasp experiments are blocked until it is repaired.
- `/joint_states.velocity` is not the derivative of position in this workcell; the E-STOP
  experiment differentiates measured positions.
- The controller rejects partial goals, which is why the safety halt fails.
- The study was performed by the same group that built the stack; there was no independent
  auditor.

## Reproduction

```bash
python3 scripts/build_numbers.py
cd publications/ieee_tro_submission
pdflatex main && bibtex main && pdflatex main && pdflatex main
```

The number generator aborts if a required summary is missing or if an experiment's rows do
not carry exactly one non-empty commit. It writes `numbers.tex`, `ledger.csv`, and the five
figures. The live raw-log directory is git-ignored; extract `data/journal/raw_logs.tar.gz`
into `data/journal/` to rebuild from a fresh clone.

## Deviations

All deviations from the pre-registration are listed in `DEVIATIONS.md`: the P0-REACH
provenance re-run, the P2-N4 target box, the paired bootstrap interval, the two N4 metrics,
the E-STOP threshold and sensitivity check, path-message matching, wall-to-sim interpolation,
the P0-CONV realized-part-speed metric, and the summary/row commit mismatch in the earlier
E-STOP run.
