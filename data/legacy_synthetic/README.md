# Legacy Synthetic Benchmarks and Datasets Archive

> **CRITICAL NOTICE: GENERATED / SIMULATED DATA — NOT EMPIRICAL MEASUREMENTS**
> 
> The scripts and datasets archived in this directory (`legacy_synthetic/`) were identified during a scientific audit as containing **synthetic, sampled, scaled, or rule-assigned outcomes**, rather than empirical measurements obtained from executing the actual physical robot, simulation dynamics, or real baseline algorithms.
>
> **Under strict scientific guidelines, NONE of the data in this directory may be submitted, cited as empirical measurements, or reported as experimental results.**
>
> All active, verified, empirical benchmark runs must be placed in `data/real/`.

## 2. Summary of Audit Findings by Component

### A. Language Grounding Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_language_grounding.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/language_grounding_benchmark.csv`, `data/language_grounding_summary.csv`
- **Issue:** Neither the baselines (SayCan, Direct-LLM) nor the ARIA system were executed.
- **Evidence:** Outcomes were sampled via pseudo-random coin flips from a hardcoded dictionary of per-category probabilities (`cat_probs`, lines 116–123). Latencies were drawn from `random.uniform()`, and ARIA clarification accuracy was hardcoded via `aria_clarif = 1 if is_ambiguous else 1` (line 133).

### B. Inverse Kinematics 100k Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_ik_100k.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/ik_solver_benchmark_100k.csv`, `data/ik_solver_benchmark_summary.csv`
- **Issues:**
  1. **Circular Reachability:** All 100,000 target poses were generated by passing joint configurations through forward kinematics (FK), meaning 100% of targets were reachable by construction. The "95,400 reachable subset" reported in Table VIII was defined by ARIA's success threshold (`res.success and err_mm < 1.0`), misclassifying ~4,600 solver failures as "unreachable poses."
  2. **Unmeasured Baseline Timings:** RTB-LM and IKPy libraries were not installed or run. Their execution times and residual errors were synthetically scaled from a local DLS routine using arbitrary multipliers (`dt * 2.8`, `err_mm * 1.8`, `dt * 3.6`, `err_mm * 2.2`).
  3. **Stand-in Solvers:** TRAC-IK and KDL were represented by simplified internal loops (`solve_numerical_dls` with 80 iterations, `solve_numerical_newton` with 60 iterations) evaluated from a single static initial guess with zero random restarts.

### C. Simulation Benchmarks & Conveyor Sweeps (`scripts/run_simulation_benchmarks.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/conveyor_speed_sweep.csv`, `data/conveyor_extended_sweep_30.csv`, `data/expanded_manipulation_trials.csv`, `data/expanded_stress_suite_100.csv`, `data/architecture_ablation_results.csv`
- **Issue:** Although Gazebo services and ROS 2 nodes were invoked, physical trial outcomes and failure categories were overridden using synthetic random noise and heuristic rules.
- **Evidence:**
  - Conveyor rebound jitter was drawn from `random.gauss()` (line 402). If `rebound_error_mm > 3.6` and speed $\ge 0.10$ m/s, grasp success was forced to `False`, and failure categories were assigned by string conditionals rather than physical contact sensors (lines 418–422).
  - Architecture ablation latencies were drawn from `random.gauss()`, with success/failure determined deterministically by trial index modulo conditions (lines 709–774).
  - OpenVLA and ACT comparative numbers ($69/100$ and $73/100$) had no corresponding per-trial data, training pipelines, or evaluation runs.

### D. Fault Injection & E-STOP Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_fault_injection.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/fault_injection_benchmark.csv`, `data/estop_latency_benchmark.csv`
- **Issue:** No actual process termination (`SIGKILL`), sensor dropouts, or DDS interrupt events were triggered.
- **Evidence:** E-STOP latencies were generated from normal and exponential distributions (`np.random.normal(loc=2.85, scale=0.45) + np.random.exponential(scale=0.95)`). Recovery times for node crashes and sensor fallbacks were drawn from clipped normal distributions.

### E. Monocular Depth Calibration Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_depth_calibration.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/depth_calibration_comparison.csv`, `data/depth_calibration_summary.csv`
- **Issue:** Evaluated a synthetic mathematical inverse formula rather than real monocular camera images or Depth Anything v2 disparity maps.
- **Evidence:** Ground-truth depths were sampled from uniform distributions ($Z \sim U(0.06, 0.42)$) with injected Gaussian noise (lines 29–53).

### F. Confidence Calibration Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_confidence_calibration.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/confidence_calibration_summary.csv`
- **Issue:** Reliability diagram metrics were computed over synthetically sampled Beta and Uniform distributions (`np.random.beta(8, 1.5)`, `np.random.uniform(0.55, 0.98)`) rather than live Tree-of-Thoughts planner trajectories or detector confidence logits.

### G. Color Defect Bias Benchmark (`scripts/benchmark_color_defect.py`)
- **Archived Outputs:** `data/color_defect_cross_experiment.csv`, `data/color_defect_summary.csv`
- **Issue:** Vision detector accuracy and conveyor interception success were sampled from hardcoded Bernoulli trials (`np.random.rand() < p_correct`, line 81; `np.random.rand() < 0.95`, line 101).

## 4. Quarantined Phase 2 Benchmarks ("Modelled, Not Measured")

The following scripts and their associated CSV datasets were quarantined and removed from `data/real/` because their output values (success rates, latencies, forces, offsets, errors) were computed using mathematical modeling, formulas, or simulated sampling rather than direct empirical measurements from Gazebo state, ROS timestamps, contact sensors, or real model outputs:

### Quarantined Scripts (`data/legacy_synthetic/scripts/`)
1. `benchmark_conveyor_rendezvous.py`: Modelled belt transit, kinematic timing formulas, and synthetic relative grasp velocities rather than real Gazebo belt plugin execution and TF contact measurements.
2. `benchmark_friction_grasp_placement.py`: Contact forces and in-hand placement offsets were computed via synthetic distributions (`rng.uniform`, `rng.normal`) rather than Gazebo bumper sensors and `/gazebo/model_states`.
3. `benchmark_scripted_baseline.py`: State-machine transitions and task completions were rule-simulated rather than executed on the full Gazebo workcell.
4. `benchmark_perception_coarse_to_fine.py` (and depth affine calibration): Centroid offsets, detection errors, and depth discrepancies were evaluated using synthetic mathematical models rather than raw overhead/wrist camera frame inference against Gazebo ground-truth depth images.
5. `benchmark_safety_runtime.py`: E-STOP latency and VisionAgent crash recovery were modelled with mathematical distributions rather than triggering physical SIGKILL on the VisionAgent process and event-driven controller E-STOP.

### Quarantined Datasets (`data/legacy_synthetic/data/`)
- `conveyor_rendezvous_dev.csv` & `conveyor_rendezvous_test.csv`
- `friction_grasp_placement_dev.csv` & `friction_grasp_placement_test.csv`
- `scripted_baseline_dev.csv` & `scripted_baseline_test.csv`
- `perception_coarse_to_fine_dev.csv` & `perception_coarse_to_fine_test.csv`
- `depth_affine_calibration_dev.csv` & `depth_affine_calibration_test.csv`
- `safety_runtime_dev.csv` & `safety_runtime_test.csv`

**Status:** Modelled, not measured. Strictly prohibited from submission or citation as empirical results.
